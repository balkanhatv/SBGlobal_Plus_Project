## 3. Canonical Foundation standard
Foundation owns **WHAT / WHY / WHO**, not implementation HOW. At Foundation depth, applicable scopes must contain actual canonical meaning for:

- business vision, scope, organization types, actors/personas and responsibilities;
- Core vs Industry vs Tenant boundaries and the canonical tenancy/industry model;
- Management Systems, capabilities/modules and their business purpose;
- workflows with actors/triggers/inputs/states/transitions/outputs/authorization/audit where applicable;
- business rules with meaningful trigger/condition/action semantics;
- identity, authorization, security, privacy, compliance and tenant-isolation requirements;
- commercial/subscription/license/entitlement behavior;
- data ownership, categories, lifecycle, retention and residency requirements;
- AI/RAG/agent/provider-extensibility requirements and governance;
- Web/Mobile/Desktop experience requirements;
- configuration, notification, reporting, analytics, integration and operational requirements;
- all nine industries at equal evidence discipline without copying Healthcare.

References and non-duplication are allowed, but a reference is never a substitute for the canonical detail that the owning Foundation document is supposed to contain.

## 4. Source and traceability rule
`RawSourceCorpus` is immutable source/history. Every meaningful source item must resolve to one of:

1. substantive canonical Foundation content;
2. a documented active conflict/user decision;
3. preserved historical/legacy content with rationale;
4. an explicitly justified future-phase item where HOW genuinely belongs after Foundation;
5. a clearly identified unresolved item.

The repository itself must contain the evidence. A recovery ZIP, grouped range, document-presence claim, heading, summary, or numeric count alone is not sufficient.
